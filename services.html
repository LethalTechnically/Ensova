What We Do

Services for power plants and industrial sites, based on the Ensova organisation profile. (Service descriptions are drafted from the profile: confirm with the client.)

Operation & Maintenance

Operation contracts and day-to-day maintenance for power plants, with trained teams who keep switchyards, boilers, turbines and auxiliary systems running safely and reliably.

  • Operation contracts
  • Field operations of plant systems
  • Switchyards, switchgear and boilers
  • Turbines, BOP, AHP and CHP
🔧

Mechanical Maintenance

Planned and routine mechanical maintenance of boilers, turbines, balance of plant and ash handling plant, carried out by experienced engineers and technicians to keep equipment dependable.

  • Boiler and turbine maintenance
  • Balance of plant (BOP)
  • Ash handling plant (AHP)
  • Conveyor systems
📅

Annual Maintenance & Turnaround

Annual rate contracts, overhauling, major breakdown repairs and annual turnaround support, planned so that plant units return to service safely and as quickly as possible.

  • Annual rate contracts
  • Annual overhauling
  • Major breakdown repairs
  • Annual turnaround
👷

Manpower Support

Supply of skilled technicians, engineers and experts for mechanical and rotary equipment maintenance, workshops and power plant operations, matched to the needs of each client site.

  • Skilled technicians
  • Engineers and experts
  • Workshop manpower
  • Power plant support
